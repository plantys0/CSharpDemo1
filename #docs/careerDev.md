### Local PhD Options in AI at Melbourne-Based Universities

Melbourne hosts several top universities with strong AI research programs, allowing you to pursue a PhD while remaining based there. As an Australian citizen, you'll qualify for domestic fees, often covered by the Research Training Program (RTP) or similar schemes, making tuition free or offset for eligible candidates. Most programs are 3-4 years full-time (or 6-8 years part-time), with flexibility for working professionals. Entry typically requires a relevant master's or honors degree with a research component (e.g., your M.S. from UCLA with GPA 3.7/4.0 should align well), plus demonstrated research potential via publications or professional experience like your AI integrations at GE Vernova.

Programs focus on areas like machine learning, deep learning, AI ethics, and applications in fields such as energy or finance, building on your background.
